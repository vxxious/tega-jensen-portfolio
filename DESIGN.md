# Design system

Tega Jensen's portfolio uses a monochrome editorial system shaped around careful evaluation: near-white paper, black ink, strict rules, sparse dot fields, and square-grid display lettering.

## Principles

- Typography carries the identity; decoration stays structural.
- AI evaluation and content quality lead the story.
- Evidence is precise, CV-supported, and easy to scan.
- Operational experience remains visible but secondary.
- Motion clarifies entry and hierarchy without delaying access.

## Visual language

- **Display:** self-hosted Handjet with a square-grid ink pattern.
- **Body:** Arial with system sans-serif fallbacks.
- **Palette:** light uses `#0c0c0c`, `#5b5b5b`, `#c7c7c7`, and `#f8f8f8`; dark reverses the system through warm white ink, charcoal paper, and muted gray rules.
- **Structure:** hairline rules, open grids, square corners, no shadows or gradients.
- **Motion:** one short headline-resolution sequence, restrained section reveals, and a bottom-up theme transition. Reduced-motion preferences keep the state change while removing spatial movement.
- **Theme control:** a header-integrated, square-cornered contrast switch persists the visitor's choice and follows the system preference on first visit.

## Responsive behaviour

Desktop preserves the measured editorial composition. Below 800px, the hero becomes a natural document flow, navigation uses a compact two-column layout, methods become a two-by-two grid, and content sections stack without horizontal scrolling.
